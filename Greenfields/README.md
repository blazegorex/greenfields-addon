# Greenfields

The guild addon for **Greenfields** on WoW: Forever. Est. Mandurah, WA 6210.

Type `/gf` to open it, or click the green shield on the minimap.

Everything is shared between guildies who run the addon, so get the whole street to install it. Stats, professions and buff info only exist for people running Greenfields.

**Residents only.** From launch, Greenfields only runs for characters in the Greenfields guild. On any other character it stays quiet, and the window just says why. It opens by itself the moment that character joins, with nothing to reload.

---

## The pages

### The Street (home)
- What's on tonight, with your signup status and one-click Yeah / Maybe / Nah.
- Who wears the crowns right now.
- The latest gossip from the Watch, who's online, and a quick camp check for your group.

### Neighbourhood Watch
A guild news feed. It posts things like dings (10, 20 ... 60), epics looted, boss kills, titles changing hands, badges, new residents and 300 professions.

### Raid Nights
- **Officers:** create raids for Barrow Deeps (10), Hyjal Summit (20), Onyxia's Lair (40) or "Other". You can repeat them weekly.
- **Everyone:** sign up as **Yeah / Late / Maybe / Bench / Nah**, pick a role and character, and add a note.
- **Roster builder (officers):**
  - Click a signed-up player, then a group slot to place them.
  - **Auto-fill** balances roles for you.
  - A buff-coverage strip shows what that lineup will be missing.
- **Raid time:**
  - **Invite signed** invites everyone who said Yeah or Late.
  - **Apply groups** sorts the raid into your lineup.
  - **Post lineup** posts the groups to raid chat.
- You get reminders 30 and 5 minutes before raids you're signed up for.

### Soft Res
- The host (an officer or the raid lead) starts a session. Everyone reserves up to the limit, which defaults to 2.
- **Adding a reserve:** pick from loot the guild has seen drop, paste an item link or ID, or shift-click an item into the box.
- **Guildies without the addon:** whisper the host `!sr [item link]`.
- **Import / export:** softres.it-style CSV import and export.
- **+N:** how many weeks in a row someone has reserved the same item without winning it.
- **Loot time:**
  - In group loot you get a popup: "You SR'd [item] - smash NEED" or "X reserved [item]. Pass it."
  - The host gets a **Loot Desk**: announce, run SR / MS / OS rolls, see live rolls, award.

### Loot Log
Who got what, how (SR / MS / OS / roll) and from which boss, plus a **Fiend Watch** leaderboard.

### Roll Call (attendance)
- Recorded automatically when the guild raids. The raid leader's addon records; if the leader doesn't run it, an officer's does.
- Snapshots are taken every 5 minutes and after every boss.
- Tracks attendance %, streaks, late arrivals (15+ minutes after the start time) and flakes (said Yeah, never showed).
- Officers can mark bench players, who count as attended.
- CSV export.

### Hall of Fame (and Shame)
32 titles that change hands as the numbers move. The current leader wears the title, once they've got a small score on it (till then it reads up for grabs); check the "This week" tab too. Some highlights:

| Title | Awarded for |
|---|---|
| **Stands In Fire** | most raid deaths |
| **Loot Fiend** | biggest loot whore |
| **Mandurah Millionaire** / **Centrelink's Favourite** | richest / poorest. The screen only shows the order, never the amount, but the score it shares gives your gold away to within about double. You can opt out in Settings. |
| **Eshay Brah** | most PvP killing blows |
| **Gravity Tester** | deaths from falling |
| **Opening Act** | first to die on a wipe |
| **Durry Break Champion** | most time AFK |
| **Been Up Since Tuesday** | most jumps |
| **Ride Or Die** | best attendance |
| **Said Yeah, Meant Nah** | most flakes |
| **On The Gear** | potions and flasks drunk |

The rest include **Uber Eats Driver**, **Mummy's Boy**, **Absolutely Rotten**, **Blue Manna Crabber** and **Happy Camper**.

Most stats also have milestone badges, and the **Sixty Club** tab lists everyone who's hit 60, in order.

### Most Wanted
When an Alliance player kills one of us, Greenfields puts their face on the poles: a wanted poster with a mugshot that matches their race, class and sex, and a price on their head.
- **Points:** 1 a body, +1 for every different guildie they've done, +1 for every dog act (a kill on someone 10 or more levels under them).
- **Packs:** jumped by a few of them? Everyone who was on you gets a poster and a point for the body: anyone who had you targeted, was your target, or shows up hitting you in the death recap. Someone just walking past doesn't.
- **It asks first:** when you're done over, the poster drops as a question. **Put them up**, or **Fair fight** if you started it. In a pack, click a face to leave them off. Ignore it and it waits at the top of the Most Wanted page. Untick "Ask before putting them up" in Settings to have them go straight up.
- **Snooze:** the Snooze button on the Most Wanted page (or on the question) gives you a break: your deaths ask nothing and wanted faces don't pop a sign. A kill on someone wanted still collects.
- **Battlegrounds and arenas:** Most Wanted sits them out. No posters, no collecting, no sightings.
- **Collecting:** whoever lands the killing blow takes every point since the last time they were sorted out. Revenge on someone who did you is worth 1 more, and anyone else who gets the honourable kill gets 1 for a hand in it. The poster gets stamped and stays up for a week.
- **The page:** Public Enemy No. 1's case file, every other poster on the wall, your nemesis, and the bounty hunters' board. Your resident file shows your points and your nemesis too.
- **In the world:** a poster drops when you're done over, a stamp slams down when you collect, enemy tooltips show the price on their head, and you get a sign when a wanted face is near you or a guildie spots one in your zone. Switch any of it off in Settings.
- **How it knows:** the game doesn't tell addons who killed you, so Greenfields watches the enemy players around you (nameplates, your target, who's got you targeted) and asks the death recap. It only names someone it has good reason to. If it gets it wrong, click **Wasn't them** on the poster, or right-click it on the page. Officers can tear a poster down for everyone.

### Level 60
Hitting 60 gets a ceremony. The screen goes dark, fireworks go up over the estuary, a fanfare plays and your 60 medal comes down.
- **Your climb:** time played, days on the road, quests handed in, deaths, jumps, a chart of every level from the day Greenfields started watching, where you came in on the street ("2nd in Greenfields to 60"), and your Most Wanted nemesis if you've got one.
- **Buttons:** **Tell the guild** puts a line in guild chat, **Frame it** takes a screenshot without the buttons, **Done** closes it (so does Esc). It never interrupts a fight: it waits until you're out of one, and a fight starting takes it down. `/gf sixty` plays it again any time.
- **Already 60?** If Greenfields saw you ding, you get the ceremony you missed, once, the next time you log in. If you were 60 before you installed it, you're still in the Sixty Club, without a date.
- **The street:** when a guildie hits 60, everyone online gets a banner across the top with fireworks, the first bars of the fanfare and a **Grats** button for guild chat.
- **Settings:** "The fanfare" (off: the game's own ding) and "Fireworks for a guildie's 60" (off: a normal sign).

### Residents
- The guild roster: mains and alts, who's online, rank, titles, attendance, professions, and who runs the addon.
- Click anyone to open their **resident file**: titles, badges, stats, roll call, trades, loot and the buffs they bring.

### Tradies (professions)
- Search "who can make X?" across the whole guild and whisper them in one click.
- Also shows everyone's profession levels and transmute / Mooncloth cooldowns.
- Open each of your profession windows once so your recipes get shared.

### Buff Check
- Shows which raid buffs your group has and which it's missing.
- For each missing buff it works out **who should drop which camp object**, which campfire tier you need, and who should cook it.
  - Example: with one paladin, the paladin does Might and Wisdom, and someone with Fishing drops a Fish Bowl for Kings.
- **Ping** buttons pop a road sign on that person's screen telling them to get their object down.
- A live scan shows who is actually missing what. It only works out of combat, because Forever hides buffs from addons in combat.
- Camp objects can't go down inside instances, so set up camp at the raid entrance.

## Installing on Windows
1. Download [GreenfieldsSetup.msi](https://github.com/blazegorex/greenfields-addon/releases/latest/download/GreenfieldsSetup.msi). That link always gets the newest version.
2. Double-click it. It isn't signed, so Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Click **Next**. It finds your WoW folder by itself; if it can't, click **Browse...** and pick the World of Warcraft folder (the one with `_forever_` inside it).
4. Leave **Also install the Greenfields Updater** and **Turn on auto updates** ticked if you want new versions to arrive by themselves. Click **Install**.

Close WoW first. If it says something's still got hold of Greenfields, close WoW and any Explorer windows showing the Greenfields folder, then click **Try again**.

To remove it: Settings > Apps > Greenfields > Uninstall. Your settings (in WoW's `WTF` folder) stay put.

## Updating
WoW won't let addons update themselves, so use the **Greenfields Updater**. The installer above can put it in your Start menu as **Update Greenfields**. Or get `GreenfieldsUpdater.zip` from the release page:

1. Unblock the zip: right-click > Properties > Unblock.
2. Unzip it somewhere permanent.
3. Double-click **Update Greenfields**. It finds WoW, downloads the latest release, checks it's genuine, and installs it.

**Turn On Auto Updates** keeps it current in the background: it checks at login and every 6 hours. **Turn Off Auto Updates** stops it. Running a newer `GreenfieldsSetup.msi` works too.

**On a Mac, or doing it by hand,** the updater won't help you, so:

1. Download [Greenfields.zip](https://github.com/blazegorex/greenfields-addon/releases/latest/download/Greenfields.zip). That link always gets the newest version.
2. Unzip it, and find the folder with `Greenfields.toc` directly inside it. Windows can put it one folder deeper than you'd think.
3. Drag that folder into `World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns`, replacing any old one.
4. Quit and reopen WoW.

Do the same again each time a new version comes out. `/gf install` gives you these links and a post to paste in Discord, for getting a mate set up.

In game, a yellow **UPDATE** pill and a toast appear when a guildie is running a newer version. After you update, restart WoW and you'll get a "What's new" popup.

## Limits worth knowing
- **No damage, healing or fire-damage meters.** Forever blocks addons from reading the combat log, so "stands in fire" is measured by raid deaths.
- **Self-reported.** Stats, professions and buff abilities only exist for people running Greenfields.
- **Not yet verifiable:**
  - Master loot handover: the Loot Desk feature-detects it, and without it the desk announces and records instead.
  - Camp buffs inside raids.
  - Raid lockouts.
  These depend on Forever details that weren't public before launch. Run `/gf doctor` once raids open on Dec 9.
- **Secret values.** In combat and boss encounters the game hides some info from addons, such as names and buffs. Greenfields waits until combat ends rather than guessing.
